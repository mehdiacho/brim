A living notch
for Windows.

Brim puts a small black pill at the top of your screen. It shows what's playing, catches your volume and battery changes, and slides your notifications in. It's free and open source.

Download for Windows MSIX package

Beta · Windows 10 and 11 (x64) · about 0.5 MB · all releases

What it does

Now playing

Works with Spotify, browsers, or any app that shows media controls. You get the album art, play/pause and skip, and synced lyrics from LRCLIB.

Volume and brightness

A slim bar slides out of the notch in place of the Windows flyout.

Battery

Charging, unplugged, low and full alerts, plus a charge ring when you hover.

Bluetooth

Shows when headphones connect or drop, with their battery level when the device reports it.

Notifications

Toasts from your apps show up in the notch, so they're easy to glance at.

Stays out of the way

Brim hides for fullscreen games and video, and stays light on CPU. It updates itself quietly.

Install

  1. Download Brim-portable.exe and put it wherever you like, for example a Brim folder in Documents. There's nothing to install.
  2. Run it. Brim isn't signed with a paid certificate yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you. Choose More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Allow notification access if Windows asks, so Brim can mirror your toasts. Right-click the pill in the tray to set up Start with Windows and the other options.

Prefer an installed app? Download Brim.cer. Right-click it, choose Install Certificate → Local Machine → Trusted People, then open Brim.msix.

FAQ

How do updates work?

Brim checks GitHub for a new release when it starts and every few hours after that. It downloads the update in the background, checks the file's fingerprint, and then tells you it's ready. One click installs it. If you'd rather it installed on its own while you're away, turn that on under Updates in the tray menu.

Does it collect anything?

No. Brim connects to two places: LRCLIB for lyrics of the song that's playing, and GitHub to check for updates. The keyboard hook only watches for the volume keys, Caps Lock and layout switches, and passes every key straight on.

How do I remove it?

Quit it from the tray menu and delete the exe. Settings live in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Brim. If you installed the MSIX, uninstall Brim from Start.

Something looks wrong

It's a beta, and reports help. Tell Mehdi what you saw. A screenshot and your Windows version and display scaling make it much easier to fix.

What it does today

Everything you glance at, in one place

All of this is in the version you can download now. Each part can be switched off from the tray menu.

Music, with lyrics

The album art, a record that turns while the song plays, and controls for any player Windows knows about.

  • Lyrics that follow the song line by line
  • A split view to scroll them, or click a line to jump there
  • Timing a little off? Nudge it, and Brim remembers

Your notifications, in the notch

Brim shows Windows' pop-ups in the notch instead of the corner of the screen.

  • Reply to a message without opening the app
  • Look back through the ones you missed
  • Do Not Disturb holds them until you're ready

Focus timer

Rounds of focus and breaks, with a ring that runs down. It shares the notch with music: stacked, side by side, or taking turns.

Water

Set a daily goal. Brim reminds you to drink through the day, and one click logs it.

To-do

Today's tasks, with times. When one is due, Brim tells you and lets you start it or put it off.

Shelf

Drag files onto the notch to keep them close, then drag them out wherever they're going.

Quick status

Volume, brightness, battery and charging, Bluetooth devices, Wi-Fi and Caps Lock, shown for a moment when they change.

Questions

Before you download

Is it really free?

Yes. There's no account, no ads and no subscription.

Which version should I get?

Most people want the portable one: download it and run it, nothing to install. The MSIX package installs Brim like a Store app, but you first need to trust Brim.cer, the certificate it's signed with.

Windows warned me about the download. Is it safe?

Brim is new and signed with its maker's own certificate rather than one Windows already knows, so SmartScreen may warn you the first time. Choose More info, then Run anyway.

Will it get in the way of my windows?

It tries hard not to. When there's a button or page under the notch it lets clicks through, and it shrinks out of the way of a window's close and maximise buttons. You can hide it any time with Ctrl+Alt+B.

Does it work with my music app?

Brim works with any player that shows up in Windows' own media controls, like Spotify, browsers and most music apps. Lyrics come from LRCLIB, a free lyrics library, when the song has them.

What does it send over the internet?

The song you're playing, to look up its lyrics; a check for new versions; and feedback, only when you send it. Your settings, to-do list and shelf stay on your PC.

How do updates work?

Brim checks for new versions itself and offers to install them. You can also choose Check for updates in the tray menu.

How do I turn something off?

Right-click Brim's icon in the system tray. Every page, reminder and status pop-up has a switch there.

Brim is a notch that sits at the top of your Windows screen. The demo below is interactive. What it shows:

  • Music, with the words to the song as it plays.
  • Notifications you can answer without opening the app.
  • A focus timer, water reminders and a to-do list for today.
  • A shelf you can drop files onto and drag them back out of.
  • Volume, brightness, Caps Lock, charging and earbuds, shown where you're looking.
  • A pair of eyes for company, and a choice of a pill or a half-sun, with ears if you like.
  • It steps aside when a window goes full screen.
